02

Selected Research

LiFT: Lifted Inter-slice Feature Trajectories for 3D Image Generation from 2D Generators

arXiv · 2026

Efficient 3D medical image synthesis by learning inter-slice feature trajectories from 2D generators, at roughly 135× lower inference cost than comparable 3D methods. Evaluated on BraTS 2023 and SynthRAD2023 across unconditional generation, missing-modality MR synthesis, and MR-to-CT translation.

An AI-Cyborg System for Adaptive Intelligent Modulation of Organoid Maturation

bioRxiv · 2024

A closed-loop platform integrating flexible bioelectronics with Gaussian Process Bayesian optimization to adaptively control electrical stimulation of cardiac organoids, reaching ~70% higher conduction velocity than fixed-parameter baselines.

Implanted flexible electronics reveal principles of human islet cell electrical maturation

Science · 2026

Tissue-like stretchable electronics implanted during organogenesis of stem cell-derived pancreatic islets enable months-long, single-cell-resolved electrophysiology, revealing two major electrical states distinguished by the glucose threshold for action potential firing.

Flexible nanoelectronics reveal arrhythmogenesis in transplanted human cardiomyocytes

Science · 2025

Ultra-flexible nanoelectronic meshes implanted alongside transplanted human iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes resolve arrhythmogenic dynamics at single-cell resolution over weeks in vivo, and show how coinjection with RADA16 promotes sarcomere organization.

CARLA Simulated Data for Rare Road Object Detection

IEEE ITSC · 2021

Uses the CARLA simulator to generate training data for rare road object detection, improving sim-to-real transfer for autonomous driving perception.
